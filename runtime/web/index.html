Explain away, then look again

A slow service makes everything that calls it look slow too. Most tools return the single most suspicious service and stop. CoRCA repeats the search on whatever its first answer cannot explain.

  1. Detect anomalies

    Compare each metric after the alert with the minutes before it.

  2. Rank the strongest root

    A proven ranker, BARO or CIRCA, picks the most likely cause.

  3. Explain away its callers

    Symptoms that service can cause upstream are set aside. Callers' own CPU and memory stay in play.

  4. Repeat until nothing is left

    Stop when no remaining anomaly clears the calibrated threshold, after at most three rounds.

More answers without more guessing

Returning a fixed top two or top three looks safe, but every extra guess is a false lead when only one service broke. CoRCA adds a cause only when the evidence is still there.

0.772F1 with BARO, against 0.587 for a fixed top two
1.3services named per single-fault incident on average, against 2 or 3 for a fixed top-k

These are single-fault results; tau is calibrated on held-out repeats. Evaluation on incidents with two and three concurrent faults is under way, so no multi-fault numbers are claimed here yet.
